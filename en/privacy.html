Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Controller

Controller responsible for the Ringjahr app under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

mip@gmx.biz

Save data: local to your device only

Ringjahr stores your save data exclusively on your device, as a JSON file in the app's application support directory. There is no server operated by the provider that save data is sent to, no accounts, and no sync through any service of ours. The provider has no access to this data.

No sign-in, no account

Ringjahr does not require registration. No account or profile data is collected.

Purchases

Any purchases within the app are processed exclusively through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Apple; the provider of Ringjahr never receives payment data. Apple's own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Advertising (planned, not yet active)

Note: This section describes a planned feature and must be finally reviewed before publication, once the integration has actually been implemented.

We plan to show advertising through Google AdMob in a future update. As of now, this integration is not yet part of the app.

Once AdMob is integrated, the app will ask for permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before the first ad is shown, in order to identify the device across apps for advertising purposes. If that permission is not granted, only non-personalized advertising will be served.

As part of serving ads, AdMob is expected to process the following data:

  • the device's advertising identifier (IDFA), if consent was given via ATT
  • device information (e.g. device type, operating system version)
  • approximate location information derived from the IP address

Google acts as an independent controller for this advertising data. Further details will be added here once the integration is complete.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights including access to data held about you, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your data, and objection to certain processing. Since Ringjahr does not use accounts and stores save data only locally, these rights mainly become relevant once the planned AdMob advertising processing is active.

Deleting your local save data

You can delete your local save data yourself at any time:

  • in the app, under Settings → Delete Progress, or
  • by uninstalling the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact:

mip@gmx.biz
